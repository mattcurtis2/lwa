Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 17, 2026

Little Way Acres (“we,” “us,” or “our”) is a family farm in Hudsonville, Michigan. This notice explains what personal information we collect on littlewayacres.com, why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and how you can ask us about your information.

This page describes our current website practices. It is not a substitute for legal advice. Some U.S. state privacy laws apply only if a business meets revenue or volume thresholds. We still offer the request channel below to anyone who wants to exercise access, deletion, correction, or opt-out rights.

We collect the categories below when you use this site, place a market order, or fill out an interest or waitlist form. We do not sell personal information, and we do not use advertising or analytics pixels on this site.

What we collect

Depending on how you use the site, we may collect:

  • Market checkout: first name, last name, email, phone, pickup location, pickup date, and the products you order.
  • Interest and waitlist forms: information you type into Google Forms linked from this site, which typically includes contact details and questions about your farm, household, or placement needs.
  • Email or phone contact: whatever you send us at the farm email or phone number listed on the site.
  • On-device cart data: product selections stored in your browser so your cart persists between visits.
  • Payment details: card information is collected by Stripe on the checkout page. We do not store full card numbers on our servers.

We do not ask for Social Security numbers, government ID numbers, precise geolocation, or health information. Google Forms used for animal placement may ask about your land, livestock, household, or experience so we can make a responsible match. We do not use that information for advertising.

How we use information

  • Fulfill farmers-market pre-orders and send order confirmations.
  • Place dogs, goats, and sheep with suitable homes and keep waitlists.
  • Answer questions and communicate about orders, litters, and farm products.
  • Process payments and help prevent fraud through Stripe.
  • Operate, secure, and maintain the website, including staff admin login.

Sharing and service providers

We share personal information with companies that help us run the farm site, only as needed for those services:

  • Stripe — payment processing on market checkout.
  • SendGrid — transactional email such as order confirmations and privacy-request notices.
  • Google Forms — interest and waitlist submissions.
  • Printify — apparel orders, which are completed on Printify’s checkout.
  • Firebase / Google Cloud — image and file hosting.
  • Our website host — serving the site and storing order records in our database.

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We do not run Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or similar advertising or analytics tags on this site. If you buy apparel, Printify’s own privacy policy applies to that checkout.

Cookies and local storage

This site uses a small number of cookies and similar technologies:

  • Admin session cookie — set only after a staff member logs in, for up to 30 days, so the admin tools stay signed in.
  • Admin sidebar cookie — remembers whether the staff sidebar is open.
  • Stripe — Stripe’s checkout script may set cookies on the payment page for fraud prevention and to complete the payment.
  • Browser local storage — stores your cart and, during checkout, the order details needed to finish payment. It also stores your cookie choice after you accept or reject non-essential cookies.

These essential tools are used to run the site and complete purchases. On your first visit we ask you to accept or reject non-essential cookies (analytics and advertising). We do not currently run advertising or analytics tags unless you choose Accept all and we later add them. You can change your choice with Cookie settings in the site footer. We also store that choice in your browser so we remember it.

How long we keep information

We keep personal information only as long as we have a reason to keep it:

  • Orders: until pickup is complete, then as long as needed for accounting, tax, dispute, and legal recordkeeping.
  • Waitlist and interest forms: until the related litter or placement process is finished, you ask us to delete the information, or it is no longer useful for follow-up.
  • Email correspondence: as long as needed to respond and keep a record of the conversation.
  • Cart data on your device: until you clear it or complete checkout.
  • Admin sessions: up to 30 days.

Payment processors and form providers keep information according to their own policies. When you ask us to delete information we control, we will delete or de-identify it unless we need to keep a record (for example, a completed order or a privacy request itself).

Children

This site is for adults buying farm products or inquiring about livestock and dogs. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child submitted information to us, email littlewayacresmi@gmail.com and we will delete it.

Security

We use HTTPS, access controls for staff admin tools, and service providers that process payments and email. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this notice when our practices change. The “Last updated” date at the top will change when we do. Continued use of the site after an update means the new notice applies to that later use.
